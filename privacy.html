Jarvis privacy policy

Last updated November 20th, 2026.

Jarvis is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's Mac and is used only by that owner. It isn't offered to anyone else.

What Jarvis reads

When the owner signs in with Google, Jarvis can read his Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Docs and Sheets. It uses that data only to answer his own requests and to prepare drafts for him to review.

Where the data goes

Google data stays on the owner's Mac, apart from the text Jarvis sends to the AI service that writes its answers. Jarvis doesn't sell, share or publish Google data, and it doesn't use it for advertising.

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Jarvis's access at any time from his Google account under Security, then Third-party apps with account access. Removing access stops all further reading.

Contact

Questions go to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
